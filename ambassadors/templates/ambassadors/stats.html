Posts to verify

A verified post credits its creator with every account that joins its city from the moment it went up until you press Stop. That is correlation: anyone joining that city while it runs counts, including people who never saw it — and nothing stops a post but the button, so a forgotten one keeps counting. Open the link and check the post is real and went up when they say. Stories expire after a day, so verify those first.

How says what the credit rests on, and they are not equal. referrer is Google Play handing the app the code from the store URL: exact, and needs nothing from the reader. clipboard is the download button leaving a token on the pasteboard for the app to read on first launch — iOS only, because Apple tells an app nothing about how it was installed. token is the app following the link after install, the strongest and the rarest. network is the server pairing a signup with a recent click from the same address: a lead, not proof, which is why those arrive flagged. None of it stops a determined person with several SIM cards — the day-{{ retention_days }} test and your approval are what sit between a signup and a payment.
