neat invites

Who is sending invite links, and what came of them. Copies is every tap of the copy button, so one person can produce several. Opens is people who landed on an invite page, counted once per visitor per day. Joins is signups the app attributed to an invite. Real counts only those that clear the same bar the ambassador payouts use: a city, at least {{ min_posts }} post, {{ min_interactions }} interactions with other people, and still opening the app on day {{ retention_days }}. Nothing is paid here, so there is no approval step — Real is simply the honest version of Joins.
