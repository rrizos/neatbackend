Turn locked cities off

Every city starts reporting itself as open, including to copies of the app already installed. Feeds open, the lock screens stop appearing, and posting to a city feed is allowed again. Phones pick it up the next time they ask the server — the map on their next visit to it, the feed on their next launch or refresh. Nobody has to update anything.

It is reversible: the current lock state of all {{ rows }} cities is written down first, and turning it back on restores exactly that.

Turn locked cities back on

Restores every city to the state it was in when the switch was thrown, and removes the rows the switch had to create. Cities that have since passed their threshold stay open, because they were unlocked on their own merits rather than by the switch.

This does not touch LOCKED_CITIES_ENABLED, and you should not either. Setting that to False empties the lock API, and an empty response means locked to the shipped app — it would lock every city on every phone, which is the opposite of what this page is for.
